- ROTTERDAM -

Rotterdam is a major port city in the Dutch province of South Holland. It is known for its modern architecture, maritime heritage, and vibrant cultural scene. The city has a rich history and is home to numerous museums, galleries, and theaters. Rotterdam is also famous for its innovative urban planning and sustainable initiatives, making it a dynamic and forward-thinking city.
